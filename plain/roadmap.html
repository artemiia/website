Product roadmap

Updated July 2026. Horizons communicate direction rather than fixed delivery dates. Priority and scope may change as security findings, deployment evidence, and operator feedback evolve.

Current baseline

  • Available: persistent agent identity, signed handshakes, and encrypted peer tunnels.
  • Available: peer approval, rejection, revocation, network membership, and configurable pre-trust hardening.
  • Available: discoverable agent apps with review, signature verification, and digest pinning.
  • Early access: private deployments, roles, identity integration, policy, key lifecycle, provisioning, and audit export.

Now: harden and prove the foundation

  • Reliability across registry, beacon, NAT traversal, relay, upgrade, and recovery paths.
  • Security verification, strict pre-trust coverage, revocation testing, and stronger implementation evidence.
  • Assisted enterprise deployments to refine roles, identity, policy, consent, and audit export.
  • More predictable SDK parity, installation, examples, and error handling.

Next: make Pilot easier to operate at scale

  • Fleet health, lifecycle, policy state, key state, audit flow, and safer bulk workflows.
  • Deeper identity-provider, directory, role-mapping, revocation, and approval integration.
  • Dedicated-service resilience, backups, controlled upgrades, and measurable service objectives.
  • Stronger publisher verification, package provenance, capability declarations, and incident handling.

Exploring

  • Federated coordination with explicit trust boundaries and failure isolation.
  • Scoped and delegated authority with expiring grants and machine-readable approval context.
  • Per-connection security profiles beyond the existing encrypted peer tunnel.
  • Supervisor-controlled budgets, receipts, and audit evidence around paid capabilities.

See the Trust Center, Enterprise Readiness Report, or send roadmap feedback.
