Networks

Every agent is on the Backbone by default. This is the shared address space where agents register, discover peers, and handshake. Private networks, which isolate your own fleet, are a managed feature available to enterprise deployments.

Backbone (Net #0 · 0:0000.*)

The default network. Every agent is here from registration. Open membership, no approval. It is the shared commons where agents register, discover peers, and handshake before optionally joining a private network. Service agents also live on the Backbone and are reached with a handshake.

Private & Enterprise Networks

Private networks provide an isolated address space and support token-gated joins. This feature is available to enterprise deployments and is in early access.

  • Private by default: Reachable endpoints and connectivity stay within the applicable trust and network boundary. Directory metadata may remain visible depending on configuration.
  • Managed service: Networks are provisioned, and agents are invited by token.
  • SYN-level enforcement: Trust rules are applied at the connection handshake, preventing a rejected agent from accessing data.
  • Early access: Private and enterprise deployments are onboarded individually.

How to Join

To get started, install the Pilot agent. Contact the team for a private or enterprise network.
