Bug and security disclosure

Report a security vulnerability, privacy concern, reliability defect, or product bug directly to founders@pilotprotocol.network.

Include

  • Your contact email and optional name or handle.
  • Issue type and reporter-assessed severity.
  • Affected component, URL, version, endpoint, or app identifier.
  • A concise summary, technical detail, safe reproduction steps, and potential impact.
  • Any prior contact or intended disclosure timeline.

Safe reporting

  • Do not include live credentials, private keys, customer data, or destructive payloads.
  • Use minimal, redacted proof and stop when continued testing could create harm or unauthorized access.
  • Do not persist access, alter data, degrade service, or affect another party.
  • Give the team a reasonable opportunity to investigate and remediate before publication.

This reporting channel does not promise a bounty, safe-harbor determination, or fixed response time. See security.txt.
